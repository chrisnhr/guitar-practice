## What it trains

An even downpicked eighth-note pulse with palm-muted roots and ringing power-chord accents. The E5, G5 and A5 shapes are familiar from the previous exercise; the new skill is controlling the contrast between short notes and open accents.

## How to practise

1. Rest the edge of your picking hand lightly near the bridge. Find a low-E note that is short but still clearly pitched.
2. Count "1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &" and downpick all eight notes in the first bar evenly.
3. In the next bars, keep the single root notes muted and briefly lift your palm for each chord on beats 1 and 3.
4. Start at 60 bpm. Raise the speed by 5% only after the pulse stays even and the chord accents ring clearly.
