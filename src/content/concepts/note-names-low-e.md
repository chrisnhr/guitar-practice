---
title: Notes on the Low E String
summary: The natural notes from open E up to the 12th fret, and why the 12th fret repeats the open string.
prereqs: []
---

The lowest string in standard tuning is **E2**. One fret up is F, then F#, G, G#, and so on. The natural notes (no sharps or flats) on that string are:

```
open  E
1     F
3     G
5     A
7     B
8     C
10    D
12    E   <- one octave above the open string
```

The distance from one fret to the next is a **semitone**, the smallest step in Western music. Two semitones make a **whole tone**. Most of the theory in this site is built from those two ideas.

The 12th fret is special: the string vibrates at exactly half its open length, so it sounds the same note an octave higher. That pattern repeats on every string.

You do not need to memorise this today. Play the notes, say their names out loud, and let the fretboard do the teaching.
