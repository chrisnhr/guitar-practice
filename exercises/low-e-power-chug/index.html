Guitar only — listen to the part.

What it trains

Downpicking stamina and clean chord changes between E5, G5 and A5. The shape stays the same; only the position changes. This is the first riff skill that transfers directly to punk and metal.

How to practise

  1. Set the player to roughly 60% and let the metronome run.
  2. Play one chord per click. Listen for the two strings ringing together, not one at a time.
  3. When four clean bars feel easy, nudge the speed up by 5%.
  4. Keep the fretting hand relaxed. If your thumb is squeezing the neck, stop and reset.

If the chord change is the problem, practise moving the shape silently between clicks before adding sound. If the picking is the problem, stay on E5 only and let the changes wait.

Theory behind it

  • Power Chords — A root and a fifth. Two notes that carry rock, punk and metal.
  • Notes on the Low E String — The natural notes from open E up to the 12th fret, and why the 12th fret repeats the open string.
