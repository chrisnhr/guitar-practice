---
title: Palm Muting
summary: Lightly rest the picking-hand edge near the bridge to shorten low-string notes into a tight rock pulse.
prereqs: [power-chords]
---

Palm muting shortens a note by resting the outside edge of your picking hand lightly on the strings near the bridge as you pick. Move your hand a little at a time until the note stays pitched but has a short, controlled sound. Too much pressure, or resting too far from the bridge, can choke the note completely.

For a riff with ringing chord accents, keep the mute on the single-note pulse and lift it briefly for the chords.
